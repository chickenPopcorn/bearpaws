# Accurate-Salamander piano credits

This piano uses **Accurate-Salamander Grand Piano 6.2a**, based on recordings of
a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

- **Alexander Holm** — original Salamander Grand Piano recordings and SFZ.
- **Chisato Yamauchi / Accurate-Salamander Project** — restoration, remastering,
  and the Accurate-Salamander soundbank and SFZ mapping.
- **Hiroharu Narikawa** — the version 6 piano retuning.

Source: [Accurate-Salamander Project](https://www.ir.isas.jaxa.jp/~cyamauch/AccurateSalamander/).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The [license and adaptation notes](./LICENSE.md) and
[original README](./source/README-upstream.txt) are included with the app.

This browser version uses eight of the original sixteen playing-intensity
layers. It preserves the original lossless, 24-bit stereo FLAC files, their
tuning, and the recommended live preset's per-key mapping. Optional mechanical
noise, sampled release, and SFZ pedal-resonance voices are not included.
