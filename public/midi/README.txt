Paw Pal beginner practice collection

These short arrangements match the Paw Pal practice library.
Each MIDI has separate left- and right-hand tracks, tempo, and one full
silent count-in bar, keeping the musical downbeats on barlines.
Finger numbers are stored as MIDI text events and in catalog.json;
Paw Pal displays the complete fingering in its built-in arrangements.
Other MIDI players may ignore text events. Reimporting a MIDI into
Paw Pal treats it as a general MIDI file, without the lesson metadata.

The catalog records countInBeats as the leading silent quarter-note beats.
Melody credits, starting positions, and per-note fingering are in catalog.json.
Dancing Raindrops is the personal-practice transcription supplied by the user.
